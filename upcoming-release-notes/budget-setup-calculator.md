Add a Budget Calculator page (`/budget-setup`) for guided monthly income planning. Select income accounts, choose an analysis period (1–3 months or custom), pick true payroll deposits to estimate monthly income, review suggested recurring bills from all accounts, and allocate savings and flexible spending by fixed dollar amounts or percentages of income/leftover. Category mapping is optional and progressive — rows without a mapping are skipped on Apply rather than blocking it. A live summary panel tracks the full budget math throughout.
